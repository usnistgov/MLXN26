AGU Conference Center

MLXN26 will be held at the AGU Conference Center in Washington, DC. The venue is located in the Dupont Circle neighborhood at 2000 Florida Avenue, NW, Washington, DC 20009, about 0.30 miles from the Dupont Circle Metro station on the Red Line.

The building is a net zero energy, LEED Platinum-certified facility with modern meeting spaces, informal gathering areas, and room for up to 175 guests. Learn more about the building on the AGU Conference Center page.

Getting There from Local Airports

Reagan National (DCA)
Directions

Take the Yellow Line from DCA to Gallery Place-Chinatown, then transfer to the Red Line toward Dupont Circle. The trip is about 22 minutes and typically costs around $2.75.

Follow airport signs for the Metro station, which is connected by covered walkways.

Dulles International (IAD)
Directions

Take the Silver Line from Dulles to Metro Center, then transfer to the Red Line to Dupont Circle. The trip is roughly 59 minutes with a similar Metro fare.

The Metrorail station connects to the terminal via a pedestrian tunnel.

Baltimore-Washington (BWI)
Directions

Take the free airport shuttle to the MARC station, ride the MARC Penn Line to Union Station (about 40 minutes), then take the Red Line to Dupont Circle. Total trip time is about 1 hour.

MARC fare is about $8 plus Metro fare.

Hotel Accommodations

A hotel block has been reserved at Residence Inn Dupont Circle, located near the symposium venue in Washington, DC.

The group rate is $239/night, excluding taxes and fees, and includes complimentary breakfast each day.

The last day to book a room at this rate is March 27, 2026.
