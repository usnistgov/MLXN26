Submit Abstract

Submit your abstract to speak at MLXN 2026. The deadline to submit is February 28th and the organizing committee will make decisions in early March.

We welcome abstracts on machine learning applications in X-ray and neutron scattering, including but not limited to:

  • • Machine learning methods for data analysis and interpretation
  • • AI-driven experimental design and optimization
  • • Deep learning for scattering pattern recognition
  • • Computational approaches combining ML with scattering techniques
  • • Real-time data processing and automation
  • • Novel applications of ML in synchrotron and neutron facilities

Both contributed talks and invited talks are welcome. Abstracts should be a maximum of 300 words.
